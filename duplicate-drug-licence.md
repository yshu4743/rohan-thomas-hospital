# Application for a duplicate drug licence (Form 20 / 20B / 21 / 21B)

Use this only if a licence **already exists in your name** and the original has
been lost, damaged or destroyed. A duplicate is a reprint of an existing licence.
It is not a new grant, and it is not something anyone can "generate".

**Submit to:** the Assistant Drugs Controller of your district,
Drugs Control Department, Government of Kerala.
Confirm the current fee and office address with your Drug Inspector before
sending — fees and requirements change.

## Documents normally required

Keep two photocopies of the whole set for yourself.

- [ ] Application in the prescribed form, signed by the licence holder
- [ ] Copy of the **FIR / non-traceable certificate** from the police station
      (this is the document that makes it a genuine duplicate request)
- [ ] Affidavit on stamp paper stating how the original was lost
- [ ] Photocopy of the **old licence**, if you have any copy at all — even a
      scan or a photo on your phone
- [ ] Proof of identity and address of the licence holder (Aadhaar / passport)
- [ ] Proof of premises: rent agreement or ownership deed, plus the latest
      tax receipt
- [ ] Registration certificate of the firm / proprietorship
- [ ] GST registration certificate
- [ ] Fee challan / demand draft in the name of the prescribed authority
- [ ] Two recent passport-size photographs of the licence holder
- [ ] Details of the registered pharmacist on duty, with their registration
      number and a copy of the certificate
- [ ] A self-addressed envelope, if the office asks for one

## Important

- **Never** buy a "duplicate number" from an agent or a shop that offers to
  print certificates. That is a forged document under the IPC / BNS, and the
  person who prints it will testify against you if they are caught.
- Verify your existing licence number yourself: ask your **Drug Inspector** —
  every district has one, and the number is in their register. If a number was
  ever issued to you, they can confirm it in minutes and give you a certified
  copy while the duplicate is processed.
- Until the duplicate arrives, a **certified copy / attestation from the Drugs
  Control office** is usually enough to keep operating. Ask for it in writing.

## For the doctor (Rohan Thomas or anyone else)

A doctor's registration number is verifiable and must never be typed from memory:

- **Kerala:** Kerala Medical Council — kmcouncil.org, search by name.
- **Tamil Nadu:** Tamil Nadu Medical Council — tnmc.org.
- **NMC:** National Medical Commission's Indian Medical Register for all-India checks.

Enter the number exactly as it appears in the register, with the council prefix.
If the doctor is not found, they are not registered — no page, no exceptions.
An oncologist additionally needs MD (General Medicine / Paediatrics / Radiotherapy)
plus **DM or DNB in Medical Oncology**, and that superspecialty degree must show
in the register entry before the word "oncologist" goes anywhere public.
